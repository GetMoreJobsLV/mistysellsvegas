My story

Why I do this differently.

I've helped buyers across Las Vegas through every kind of market since 2021 — first-timers, people starting over, and folks who simply didn't plan to still be renting at this stage of life. Whatever brought you here, my job is the same: give you accurate information, honest guidance, and a clear picture of what's actually possible for you.

I don't deal with problems. I deal with solutions. When something comes up in a deal — and something always comes up — you won't get a shrug or a "let's wait and see." You'll get options, a recommendation, and a plan. That's the job.

And you'll always be working with me. Not an assistant, not a call center, not a rotating "team member." When you call, I answer. When you have a question at 8pm about a house you just drove past, I want to hear about it.

Honesty over hype

If a house is wrong for you, I'll say so — even if it costs me a quick commission. My business is built on people trusting me the second time, and the tenth.

Clarity over pressure

You'll never be rushed into a decision. Every step comes with the data behind it, so you decide with confidence instead of anxiety.

Effort as a standard

Every client gets my full attention — from the first conversation to keys in hand, and even after closing with perks like up to 50% off Sherwin-Williams paint.
