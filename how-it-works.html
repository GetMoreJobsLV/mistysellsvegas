How it works

A clear path from "maybe someday" to keys in your hand.

No pressure, no jargon, no feeling like you're supposed to already know this. Here's exactly what working together looks like.

1

Get the free kit

Start with the Home Buyer's Unfair Advantage Kit. Five tools that show you where you stand, what a home should cost, and whether now is the right time — before you talk to anyone. It's yours instantly, no strings.

2

We talk — one honest conversation

When you're ready, we have a real conversation about your situation: your budget, your timeline, what changed, and what you actually want. No sales pitch. If buying isn't right for you yet, I'll tell you that.

3

We build your plan

I map out your specific path — what to do first, what neighborhoods fit, what to expect, and how long it really takes. You'll always know the next step, because I'll be the one walking it with you.

4

We tour, compare, and score

We look at homes that actually fit — not everything on the market. Using the Home Scorecard System, we compare them honestly so "good enough" and "great" stop blurring together, and you never wonder if you missed something better.

5

We write a smart offer

When you find the one, I pull the comps, run the numbers, and write an offer designed to get accepted without overpaying. You decide with data in front of you — not pressure.

6

I guide you to closing — and past it

Inspections, financing, paperwork, deadlines — I handle the moving parts and keep you updated at every stage. And when it's time to make it yours, my clients get up to 50% off Sherwin-Williams paint to start fresh.
